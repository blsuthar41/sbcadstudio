Who We Are

A Dedicated Shop Drawing & 3D Visualization Partner

SB Cad Studio is a technical drafting and visualization studio built for architects, interior fit-out companies, contractors and joinery workshops. We convert design intent — sketches, tender drawings or BOQs — into fabrication-ready shop drawings and photorealistic 3D visuals.

Our team works to both Indian project conventions and Gulf municipality/fit-out standards, which means fewer format corrections and faster approvals whichever market you build in.

  • Experienced in India (Rajasthan, Maharashtra, Delhi-NCR) project workflows
  • Familiar with UAE, KSA, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait & Bahrain fit-out norms
  • Dedicated point of contact for every project, not a shared queue
