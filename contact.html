Frequently Asked Questions

What file formats do you deliver?

We deliver drawings in DWG and PDF, and 3D renders in high-resolution JPG/PNG, with source files available on request.

Do you work with both Indian and Gulf drawing standards?

Yes — our team regularly prepares drawings for Indian municipal formats as well as UAE, KSA, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait and Bahrain submission standards.

How do I send you my project files?

You can share files directly over WhatsApp or email, or send a cloud drive link — whichever is easiest for you.

What is your typical turnaround time?

Turnaround depends on project scope, but most drawing packages are delivered within 3–10 working days, with express options available.

Do you offer revisions after delivery?

Yes, revisions are included until final sign-off as agreed in the project scope.
